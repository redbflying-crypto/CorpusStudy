# Events

* [The Crucifixion](crucifixion.md) - The death of Jesus Christ on the cross at Golgotha under Pontius Pilate, where he bore the sins of his people. (draft)
* [The Exodus](exodus.md) - God's deliverance of Israel from slavery in Egypt through the Passover and the crossing of the sea, the great act of redemption in the Old Testament. (draft)
* [The Fall](fall.md) - The first sin of Adam and Eve in Eden, by which sin and death entered the world, met at once by God's promise of a Redeemer. (draft)
* [The Resurrection of Christ](resurrection.md) - Jesus Christ's bodily rising from the dead on the third day, which declares him the Son of God, secures the justification of his people and pledges their own resurrection. (draft)
