# Parables

* [The Good Samaritan](good-samaritan.md) - Jesus' parable of a despised Samaritan who cares for a wounded traveller that a priest and a Levite passed by, told to answer a lawyer who asked who his neighbour was. (draft)
* [The Parable of the Sower](sower.md) - Jesus' parable of seed falling on four kinds of ground, which he himself explains as four ways of hearing the word of the kingdom. (draft)
* [The Prodigal Son](prodigal-son.md) - Jesus' parable of a wasteful younger son welcomed home by his father and an elder brother who resents the welcome, told to answer those who murmured that he received sinners. (draft)
