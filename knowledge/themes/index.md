# Themes

* [Covenant](covenant.md) - The binding relationship God establishes with his people by promise and oath, unfolding from Noah and Abraham to the new covenant in Christ's blood. (draft)
* [Creation](creation.md) - God's free act of making all things out of nothing by his Word, very good, for his glory, and his purpose to renew what sin has marred. (draft)
* [Grace](grace.md) - God's free and undeserved favour toward sinners in Christ, the source of salvation from election to glory. (draft)
* [Holiness](holiness.md) - God's utter majesty and moral purity, and the consecration to him he requires of and works in his people. (draft)
* [Law and Gospel](law-and-gospel.md) - The distinction and harmony between God's law, which commands and condemns, and the gospel, which freely gives in Christ the righteousness the law requires. (draft)
* [Sin](sin.md) - Rebellion against God and transgression of his law, which entered through Adam, corrupts every part of human nature and brings death. (draft)
* [The Kingdom of God](kingdom-of-god.md) - God's sovereign reign over all things, promised to David's son and inaugurated by Christ, already present by Word and Spirit and awaiting its completion at his return. (draft)
* [Worship](worship.md) - The honour, adoration and service God's people render to him alone, in spirit and in truth, in the way he has appointed. (draft)
