# People

* [Abraham](abraham.md) - The patriarch whom God called out of Ur, with whom he made his covenant of promise, and whose faith Scripture holds up as the pattern of justifying faith. (draft)
* [Adam](adam.md) - The first man, made in God's image, whose disobedience brought sin and death on the human race and whom Paul sets beside Christ as a figure of him. (draft)
* [David](david.md) - The shepherd anointed king of Israel, psalmist and recipient of God's covenant promise of an everlasting throne, fulfilled in Christ the Son of David. (draft)
* [Elijah](elijah.md) - The prophet who called Israel back from Baal to the LORD in the days of Ahab, was taken up to heaven, and was promised as the forerunner fulfilled in John the Baptist. (draft)
* [Isaiah the prophet](isaiah.md) - Isaiah son of Amoz, the prophet called by a vision of the holy God, who counselled the kings of Judah from Uzziah to Hezekiah and called them to trust the LORD rather than Assyria or Egypt. (draft)
* [Jesus Christ](jesus.md) - Jesus of Nazareth, the eternal Son of God made man, who lived, died, rose and ascended for his people, and is the one mediator between God and men as prophet, priest and king. (draft)
* [John the Baptist](john-the-baptist.md) - The prophet sent before Jesus to call Israel to repentance, baptize, and point to Christ as the Lamb of God; the promised Elijah who prepared the way of the Lord. (draft)
* [Moses](moses.md) - The prophet through whom God delivered Israel from Egypt and gave the law at Sinai, read in Reformed teaching as a faithful servant and a type of Christ. (draft)
* [Paul](paul.md) - Saul of Tarsus, the Pharisee and persecutor whom the risen Christ called to be apostle to the Gentiles, and whose letters set out the gospel of grace that shaped the Reformation. (draft)
* [Peter](peter.md) - Simon Peter, the fisherman called to be an apostle, who confessed Jesus as the Christ, denied him, was restored, and became a leading preacher of the early church. (draft)
