# Books of the Bible

* [Acts](acts.md) - Luke's account of how the risen Christ, by his Spirit, spread the gospel through the apostles from Jerusalem to Rome. (draft)
* [Ephesians](ephesians.md) - Paul's prison letter on God's eternal purpose to unite a people in Christ, saved by grace, and on the life worthy of that calling. (draft)
* [Exodus](exodus.md) - The book of Israel's deliverance from Egypt, the covenant and law given at Sinai, and the building of the tabernacle where God's glory dwelt. (draft)
* [Genesis](genesis.md) - The book of beginnings, telling of creation, the fall and the flood, and of God's covenant promises to Abraham, Isaac, Jacob and Joseph. (draft)
* [Hebrews](hebrews.md) - An anonymous "word of exhortation" showing that the Son, God's final word, is the better high priest of a better covenant, and urging believers to hold fast. (draft)
* [Isaiah](isaiah.md) - The prophecy of Isaiah son of Amoz, announcing judgment on Judah and the nations and comfort through a coming King and a suffering Servant. (draft)
* [John](john.md) - The fourth Gospel, written so that readers may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the eternal Word made flesh, and have life through his name. (draft)
* [Matthew](matthew.md) - The first Gospel, presenting Jesus as the promised Messiah, son of David and son of Abraham, who fulfils the Scriptures and proclaims the kingdom of heaven. (draft)
* [Psalms](psalms.md) - Israel's inspired book of prayers and praises, used by the church in every age and read in the New Testament as speaking of Christ. (draft)
* [Romans](romans.md) - Paul's letter to the church at Rome, setting out the gospel of God's righteousness revealed in Christ and received by faith, for Jew and Gentile alike. (draft)
