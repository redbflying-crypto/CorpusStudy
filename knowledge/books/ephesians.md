# Overview

Ephesians opens in praise to the God "who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ".[^kjv-eph1-3] It sets out God's purpose to gather Jew and Gentile into one body in Christ, then calls the church to live by it. Matthew Henry says its design is "to give exalted views of the love of God, and of the dignity and excellence of Christ".[^henry-intro]

# Setting

The letter is from "Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, to the saints which are at Ephesus".[^kjv-eph1-1] He writes as "the prisoner of Jesus Christ for you Gentiles"[^kjv-eph3-1] and "an ambassador in bonds", sending news by Tychicus.[^kjv-eph6-20] Henry states, "This epistle was written when St. Paul was a prisoner at Rome."[^henry-intro]

Scholars differ here. Some early manuscripts lack the words *at Ephesus*, so many think it was a circular letter for several churches. Many critical scholars doubt that Paul wrote it, while Reformed and other traditional readers hold to his authorship. Some place the imprisonment somewhere other than Rome. (Orientation, not drawn from the corpus.)

# Outline

The turn comes at 4:1: "walk worthy of the vocation wherewith ye are called".[^kjv-eph4-1]

- **1:** blessings in Christ; prayer.
- **2:** saved by grace; Jew and Gentile made one.
- **3:** the mystery revealed to Paul.
- **4:1-16:** unity and gifts in the body of Christ.
- **4:17-5:21:** the new life.
- **5:22-6:9:** duties in the household.
- **6:10-24:** the armour of God; farewell.

# Themes

- **Election and grace.** God "hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy".[^kjv-eph1-3] In Reformed reading, Calvin calls eternal election "The ground and first cause of our calling". He adds that "election, which is the cause of good works, does not depend upon men".[^calvin-eph1] Henry puts it this way: "All who are chosen to happiness as the end, are chosen to holiness as the means."[^henry-eph1] Salvation is "by grace ... through faith; and that not of yourselves".[^kjv-eph2-8] See [election](/doctrines/election.md) and [grace](/themes/grace.md). Arminian readers ground the election of 1:4 in God's foreknowledge of who would believe. (Orientation, not drawn from the corpus.)
- **One new people.** Christ "is our peace, who hath made both one, and hath broken down the middle wall of partition".[^kjv-eph2-14] Henry calls him "the author, centre, and substance" of their peace with God and "of their union with the Jewish believers in one church".[^henry-eph2]
- **Christ and the church.** Husbands are to love their wives "even as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it"; marriage is "a great mystery" that speaks "concerning Christ and the church".[^kjv-eph5]
- **Spiritual warfare.** "Put on the whole armour of God".[^kjv-eph6-11] For Henry, "The righteousness of Christ, imputed to us, is a breastplate against the arrows of Divine wrath."[^henry-eph6]
